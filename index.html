Scan

Paper in.
PDF out.

Scan receipts, letters, contracts and forms. The camera finds each page’s edges and straightens it.

  • Capture many pages into one PDF, then reorder, rotate, crop or retake any of them.
  • Color, grayscale or black & white, on A4, US Letter or fit-to-image pages.
  • Turn photos from your library or Files into a PDF.
Edit text Pro

Edit the text itself

Double-tap a paragraph or a table cell and type. Fix a date, a name or a price in seconds.

  • Choose the font, size, bold, italic, underline, alignment and color.
  • The old words are removed from the page, not hidden, so they can’t be found or copied later.
  • On a scanned page, the old words are covered with the page’s color.
Fill & Sign

Fill it in.
Sign it once.

Type into form fields, check boxes and choose options. On scanned or flat forms, add text and check marks wherever you need them.

  • Draw your signature once and reuse it, with the date beside it if you like.
  • Place a picture, such as a stamp or logo, then move or resize it.
Text recognition Pro

Find any word, even in scans

Text recognition makes scanned pages searchable, so you can find, select and copy their words. They stay searchable in other apps too.

  • Reads English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and more.
  • Runs on your device. Your pages never leave it.
